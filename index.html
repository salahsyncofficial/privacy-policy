Salah Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Salah Sync ("the app", "we") helps Muslims track prayer times, scan masjid timetables, and stay connected with family and friends in worship. This policy explains what data the app collects and why.

Data we collect

Account information — When you create an account we store your email address, display name, and (if you choose one) a username. These are needed to sign you in and to let friends find you in the Friends Circle.

Prayer times and tracking — Your saved prayer timetable and your salah tracking history (which prayers you marked as completed) are stored in our database so they sync to your device. If you join a Friends Circle or Family Plan, the friends/family you approve can see your prayer completion status (never your other data).

Photos and PDFs you scan — When you scan a masjid timetable, the image is processed on your device to read the times. If you choose to save the timetable, the photo and extracted times may be uploaded to our community masjid database to help other users at the same masjid.

Location — Used only when you tap a feature that needs it: the Qibla compass (direction calculation, performed entirely on your device, never sent anywhere). Location is not collected in the background. When you save a masjid timetable, the masjid address you type is turned into map coordinates and a city, country and postcode by your phone's built-in map service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone). Only the address you typed is sent, never your name or account details.

Purchase information — Subscriptions are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details.

Shop orders — When you place an order through the Shop, we collect your shipping address (name, address, postcode, country, phone) solely to fulfil that order. Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see or store raw card numbers; Stripe's payment form captures card details directly between your device and Stripe's secure servers. Your shipping address is shared with the third-party fulfilment partner (e.g. CJ Dropshipping, Spocket, or another supplier) who is fulfilling your order, solely for delivery purposes. International orders may be subject to customs duties or import taxes payable by you on delivery — we show an estimate at checkout for informational purposes only; we do not calculate, collect, or remit these charges ourselves.

Crash data — We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash reports so we can fix problems.

Activity log — We keep an internal record of certain account events (account creation/deletion, username changes) and timetable scan attempts, including the scanned photo and technical diagnostic details for scans that don't extract times reliably. This is used solely to maintain your account and improve scan accuracy. You can view your own activity log inside the app; it is never shown to other users, including friends or family members.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not collect location in the background.
  • We do not read your photos except the ones you explicitly choose to scan.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, and Authentication), protected by security rules so each user can only access their own data and what friends/family explicitly share.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account, prayer times, tracking history, username, and friend connections.

We keep a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your email address after deletion so the same email address cannot claim a second free trial. The hash cannot be reversed to your email address.

You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Our full Terms of Service are also available.

Children

The app is not available to children under 13, and we do not create accounts for them, even with parental consent. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will note the new date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
